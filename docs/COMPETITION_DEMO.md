# Competition Demo Runbook

## Goal

Demonstrate one complete emergency story reliably in 3–5 minutes instead of browsing unrelated features.

## Preflight

1. Confirm CI is green.
2. Open Shift and pass audio, browser notification, API/Redis/realtime readiness.
3. Open Integrations and verify configured demo-safe probes.
4. Open Demo and reset the deterministic TEST scenario.
5. Keep a recorded fallback demo and screenshots available.

## Recommended story

1. **Problem / safety architecture** — explain that explicit SOS is stored locally first and AI cannot block it.
2. **Protected journey / SOS** — show the TEST volume-button incident.
3. **Incident Command** — show last confirmed location and contact status.
4. **Intelligence** — open the risk explanation, contributing signals and shadow assessment.
5. **Search** — show confirmed point, deterministic corridor and predicted areas as separate visual concepts.
6. **Trusted Guardian** — advance the scenario to Guardian acknowledgement.
7. **Responder** — dispatch/advance Unit 04 and show location/ETA.
8. **Evidence / communications** — show preserved evidence metadata and chronological communications.
9. **Resolution** — report person located and resolve.
10. **Reports** — show that TEST incidents do not contaminate live KPIs.

## Offline demonstration

If using a physical test handset:
- disable connectivity;
- trigger a TEST SOS;
- show local saved/pending state;
- restore connectivity;
- show upload and monitoring arrival.

Do not stage a real emergency or notify real third parties.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim:
- independent penetration testing unless completed;
- measured Safe Word accuracy unless benchmarked;
- police/EMS integration without an agreement;
- AI can determine that a kidnapping occurred;
- predicted search zones are confirmed locations.

## Judge questions

Prepare concise evidence-based answers for:
- what happens without internet;
- false alarms;
- privacy/POPIA;
- how AI is used safely;
- monitoring/responder operating model;
- first target market and business model;
- technical defensibility/IP;
- provider/infrastructure reliability;
- pilot metrics and rollout.
